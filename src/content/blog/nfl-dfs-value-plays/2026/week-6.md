## FAQ

### Why isn't every high-value name on this board a pick?

Because value only measures points against salary, it doesn't know whether a player's current role actually supports getting those points. This week's board is checked against team-reported depth-chart data before any name above gets called a starter. Anthony Richardson Sr., above, is the clearest example this week: a strong number attached to a backup who doesn't see the field unless the starter ahead of him gets hurt.

### Does an injury designation change the numbers?

Yes. A Questionable, Doubtful, or Out status pulls a player's projection down in this site's model, and an Out or Doubtful designation excludes a player from this board outright regardless of ranking basis. Any remaining designation is shown as a plain label so you can weigh it yourself.

### Why do some players show such a large gap against DK's own FPPG?

DK's FPPG is a simple recent-average number with its own limitations. It can lag a role change, overweight one big or one bad recent game, or use a shorter window than this site's model. Neither number is automatically right, a large gap is a signal worth investigating, not an error to explain away.

### When does this get updated before kickoff?

Whenever a salary, projection, or depth-chart change is significant enough to matter. A note at the top of the post marks any update after publication; no note means the numbers above are current as of publication.
