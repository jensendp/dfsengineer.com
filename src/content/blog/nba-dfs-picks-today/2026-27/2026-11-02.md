## FAQ

### What does the 80% range mean?

The range runs from the 10th to the 90th percentile of past misses at the same projection level. About four in five outcomes should land inside it and about one in five fall outside, above or below. A player projected at 32.5 with a range of 18.5 to 46.7 can finish outside that window without the projection being wrong. So far the actual results have landed inside the range 77% of the time.

### Why do a player's minutes change when a teammate is out?

The model starts from a recent-average baseline and adjusts it for how many rotation minutes teammates are newly missing. Those minutes go to the players who remain, and the size differs for bench players, role players and high-minute players. Tonight Indiana's players have 91.1 teammate minutes newly out, and Jalen Slawson and Larry Nance Jr. are each expected 6.7 minutes above their weighted averages, while the other board picks sit within about 0.6.

### How are the projections made and graded?

Projected points equal points per minute over the last 30 games times expected minutes. Expected minutes start from a recent-average baseline and shift with teammate absences. Projections are saved before lock and later graded against actual results, including the value board shown above. Early in the season the inputs come from last season, and only 13 nights have been graded so far.
